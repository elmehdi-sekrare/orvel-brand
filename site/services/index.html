Before you ask

Anything else, ask a partner directly: elmehdisekrare@gmail.com

  • A fixed fee for each phase, set once discovery is done and we both know what the project really is. Discovery itself is two paid weeks for £8k, credited if you go ahead. We invoice monthly against the plan, so the money follows the work.

  • Two rounds of revisions are built into every phase. Anything new, a market, a product line, a second site, is priced in a one-page change note before we start it. You decide, not a timesheet.

  • £1,200 for a partner and £950 for a senior designer or engineer. We use them for small pieces of work after launch, never to top up a fixed fee.

  • Photography, typefaces, music and media buying, at cost and quoted up front. Everything is licensed in your company’s name.

  • Thirty days of support are included. After that most clients keep a retainer, from £6k a month for four studio days, booked a quarter at a time with thirty days’ notice. Unused days roll over for one month, and you keep the same people.

  • Yes. About a third of our clients are pre-launch or have just raised. We usually start with naming and positioning and keep the first phase small, so the brand can grow with the company rather than ahead of it.

  • Often. We handed design systems to in-house teams at Corso and Kinro, and worked next to a performance agency on Halfpace. Who owns what is agreed in week one and written into the brief.

  • We don’t do free creative pitches: the work would be a guess. We meet, send a proposal with a fixed fee per phase, and if you want proof first, the paid discovery is the honest version of a pitch.

  • You do, in full, once the final invoice is paid. You get every working file, not just the exports.

  • Yes. Seven engineers across London and Lisbon build everything we design, usually on Sanity, Shopify or Webflow. We don’t outsource build.
