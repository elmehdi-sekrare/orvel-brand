noturna

Client
Noturna
Sector
Dance festival
Year
2025
Duration
12 weeks · 3 people
Disciplines
Festival identity, Motion, Wayfinding

Lisbon’s nine-night dance and music festival, with an identity that turns like its performers.

A poster can only stand still. So we designed an identity that turns, and made the festival look the way it sounds.

The brief

In six years Noturna grew from one warehouse in Marvila to forty venues across the city. Each year a different artist drew the poster, so nobody recognised it from one summer to the next.

Audiences also got lost. Moving between venues at two in the morning, they could not tell a Noturna door from any other club.

The idea

The wordmark is a performer. It turns, splits and settles in time with the music, and each of the nine nights has its own movement.

The same turns became the wayfinding: red flags angled towards the next venue, numbered by night, readable from the end of a street.

What we made

  • Festival identity and motion system
  • Nine nightly title sequences
  • Posters, programme and tickets
  • Wayfinding for forty venues
  • Trailer and social kit

One red, on stage and on paper

The lighting gels and the poster ink were matched to the same red, so a photograph from the stage needs no grading before it becomes a poster the next morning.

Nine nights on one wall

Every night the crews pasted over the whole wall with that night’s poster. Same red, same name, a new movement. Scroll through the week.

How the mark moves

Each letter turns on its own axis, a beat behind the one before, like a line of dancers. Slowed down, it opens every night’s title sequence.

What changed

  1. 9 days

    sold out a week before opening

    For the first time in the festival’s history.

  2. 40

    venues, one system

    Each one marked with the same red flag.

  3. 1.2m

    views of the trailer in its first fortnight

    With no paid promotion.
