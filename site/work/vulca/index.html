VULCA

Client
Vulca
Sector
Skincare
Year
2024
Duration
20 weeks · 3 people
Disciplines
Packaging, Identity, Shop

Mineral skincare from the Azores, packaged to glow on a shelf and photograph well on a phone.

The water comes up hot through volcanic rock. We wanted the bottle to hold light the way the rock holds heat.

The brief

Vulca is a small laboratory on São Miguel making serums with thermal spring water. The formulas were excellent; the packaging was a stock bottle with a printed label, and it disappeared on a shelf next to brands with fifty times the budget.

The goal was European stockists, concept stores and pharmacies, and a shop that could sell on its own.

The idea

Amber glass, a copper-coloured cap and nothing printed on the front but the name. Most people now meet a product on a phone before they hold it, so we designed the pack for a three-centimetre thumbnail first and a shelf second.

We built the range in 3D before tooling and lit it like the crater at dusk, so the same file made the renders, the shop and the factory drawings.

What we made

  • Identity and wordmark
  • Bottle, carton and dropper
  • 3D renders and product film
  • Shopify store
  • Stockist kit and shelf talkers

Designed for the thumbnail

Nothing on the front but the name. At three centimetres on a phone screen the amber glass and the copper cap do the recognising; the type only has to confirm it.

From the drawing to the glass

One file made the factory drawings and the renders. Scroll to light the range the way we lit it: like the crater at dusk.
