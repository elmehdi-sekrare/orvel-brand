Halfpace

Client
Halfpace
Sector
Running apparel
Year
2025
Duration
14 weeks · 3 people
Disciplines
Identity, E-commerce, Campaign

A Bristol running label that wanted to look as fast as it feels, from the shop to the start line.

Most running brands sell effort. Halfpace makes kit for the easy miles, so the brand had to look faster than it feels.

The brief

Two founders, a family-run factory in Porto and a club following that queued for every drop. But the website converted at 0.9% and 71% of revenue went through two marketplaces that took a third of every sale.

The brief was to move customers to the founders’ own shop before the spring range, without losing the scrappy club feeling that made people care.

The idea

Everything leans. Big Shoulders, extra bold, set at the angle of a runner’s torso at the pace the type describes. Photography was shot at real running pace on the seafront at Clevedon, no rigs and no retouching of sweat.

The shop is built around the question runners actually ask, which is not what colour but will it fit.

What we made

  • Identity refresh and type system
  • Shopify store, design and build
  • Size and fit tool
  • Spring campaign: outdoor, social, film
  • Packaging and swing tags
