Halyard Press

Client
Halyard Press
Sector
Independent publisher
Year
2022
Duration
16 weeks · 3 people
Disciplines
Identity, Website, Series design

An independent publisher in Leith, its books joined by one line.

Twelve books a year, and twelve unrelated covers. We gave them one line and let the shelf do the rest.

The brief

Halyard Press publishes twelve books a year from a first-floor office in Leith, almost all of them fiction translated from Nordic and Baltic languages. Each cover had been commissioned from a different illustrator, so the list never looked like a list.

Bookshops shelved the titles by author, scattered across the fiction section, and only 310 readers took the annual subscription. The founders wanted a press people could recognise across a shop, and a website that sold the year rather than the single book.

The idea

One red rule sits 62 mm from the foot of every cover and runs across the spine. Shelved side by side, the books join into a single line. It is the halyard, the rope that raises the sail, and it holds the list together.

Above the line, a colour for each language of origin, eight in all, and the title set in Fraunces at one size on every book. The website sells the year first: twelve books, posted as they come out.

What we made

  • Identity and wordmark
  • Series design for covers and spines
  • Colour system by language of origin
  • Website and subscription, designed and built
  • Bookshop window kit

How the mark moves

The wordmark turns like a page: each letter tips forward on its spine and settles flat. It runs on the website whenever a new book is announced.

The line joins up

Every cover and spine is printed from one template, so the rule lands at the same height to the millimetre. Shelve the year together and twelve books read as one line.

What changed

  1. 1,140

    subscribers to the year of books, up from 310

    Two years after the first books in the new series came out.

  2. 64

    independent bookshops stocking the whole series

    Many shelve it face out as one run, the line unbroken.

  3. 3

    reprints in the first year

    Against one reprint in the five years before.
