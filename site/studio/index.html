Why we work this way

Anjali Rao started Orvel in 2013 with two clients and a borrowed room on Great Sutton Street.1 The idea was simple: the identity, the website and the product should be made by the same people, in the same room.

1Great Sutton Street, Clerkenwell. We are two floors up from that first room now, above the same print shop.

In 2019 Tomás Ferreira opened Lisbon,2 after two seasons of working on Noturna from a café on Rua da Boavista. Same hours, same tools, one team.

2Rua da Boavista 72, third floor. Twelve people and the best light in the company.

Most brands now live on a screen, in motion, before they ever reach print. So we design the moving version first:3 the mark that turns, the page that loads, the screen that confirms a payment. If it holds up there, it holds up everywhere.

How we work

0102030405/ 05

  1. A partner on every project.

    The person who pitches your project runs it, from the first call to launch. Not a hand-over to whoever happens to be free.

  2. Thursday is review day.

    Every client sees work every Thursday, finished or not. Nothing waits for a big reveal, so nothing surprises you at the end.

  3. The moving version first.

    A new mark is tested on a phone, in motion, before it ever appears in a PDF. If it cannot turn, split and settle, it is not finished.

  4. Decisions get written down.

    One shared log, one line per decision. Nobody re-opens week three in week nine, including us.

  5. Home by six.

    We plan for normal hours. Weekend work is a planning failure, and we treat it like one, starting with the timeline.
